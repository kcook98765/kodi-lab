For an intentional running handoff, Advanced administrator keep-awake settings accepts zero to end your own hold and recent use. That alternative leaves Kodi running; it does not clear another client's hold or prove Stop.

The replacement is not automatically attached to an old container-bound development session. Fully automatic agent reattachment requires a separately reviewed coordinator; these existing setup steps preserve exact identity and workspace authority.
